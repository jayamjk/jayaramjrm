01 / EMPATHISE

User interviews

Warm Up

Setting the scene

  • How often do you send or receive parcels in a month?
  • What kinds of things do you usually send — work, personal, or products you sell?
Current Behaviour

The real workflow

  • Walk me through the last time you sent a parcel — every step.
  • How did you choose the courier service you used?
  • How did you know it was delivered?
Pain & Frustration

Where it breaks

  • Tell me about a time a delivery went really badly.

Expectations & Workarounds

Trust & backup plans

  • What would make you trust a new delivery service you've never used?
  • If your usual courier wasn't available right now, what would you do?

03 / DEFINE

Affinity mapping: five clusters, ranked

“Where is my parcel right now?”

Priority 16 observations
  • Tracking stalled 24 hours — a “black hole.” Felt anxiety even when the parcel was fine.
  • The gap between “out for delivery” and “delivered” is the most stressful moment.
  • Parcel sat in the destination city for days. Zero updates, no information at all.
  • When tracking stops, you don't know if it's safe, delayed, or lost.
  • Tracks obsessively until it says “Delivered” — then needs the receiver to confirm too.
  • Needed four separate confirmations before feeling certain the parcel was safe.

“I don't trust strangers. I trust people I know.”

Priority 16 observations
  • Chose a courier purely on a recognizable blue-and-yellow logo — no research.
  • Would rather pay more for peace of mind than risk an unfamiliar service.
  • Only trusts a new service when a friend personally recommends it.
  • Even buyers panic when tracking looks suspicious — trust is fragile on both sides.
  • Pays extra for a trusted courier when shipping something important.
  • Trusts new services mainly through positive reviews and word of mouth.

“When things go wrong, nobody helps me.”

Priority 25 observations
  • Vinyl delivered to the wrong building; recipient went door to door to find it.
  • Support = automated loops, a 3-day wait, and an unhelpful email.
  • Pickup delayed twice — only generic copy-paste replies, no real help.
  • A helmet arrived damaged; the courier blamed the sender's packaging.
  • The insurance and claims process was confusing and painfully slow.

“The price is never what it seems.”

Priority 24 observations
  • Shipping ₹150–200 on a ₹500 sale kills the buyer's interest entirely.
  • Checks three courier apps before every booking just to compare prices.
  • International customs leaves the recipient with a surprise bill.
  • Wants real-time pricing — no hidden surcharges — shown before committing.

“Packing it is its own nightmare.”

Priority 34 observations
  • Spent two days just finding the right box before touching the booking.
  • Worried about too little cushioning, or accidentally breaking a shipping rule.
  • Fragile items cause huge stress; overpacks because couriers handle parcels roughly.
  • Packed a helmet properly — the courier still blamed the packaging when it broke.
04 / DEFINE

Meet Arjun

A

Arjun Kumar

28 · Chartered Accountant
+ Part-time Online Seller
Very comfortable with technology. Low patience for bad UX. Compares three apps before every single booking.

His Goals

Ship fast, know it arrived safely, and never have a buyer complain about a missing delivery.

What He Sells

Bike gear, helmets and gadgets through Instagram and online marketplaces.

His Behaviour

Books from his phone only, sends the tracking ID immediately, overpacks everything, and checks obsessively.

His Frustrations

Tracking goes silent, pricing is unpredictable, damaged items get blamed on him, and support is useless.

“My customer doesn't know it's the courier's fault. They only know they ordered from me and it didn't come. So their problem becomes my problem.”
